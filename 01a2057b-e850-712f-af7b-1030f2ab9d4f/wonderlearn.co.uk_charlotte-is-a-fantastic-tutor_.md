# Charlotte is a fantastic tutor

by [Rob Watson](https://wonderlearn.co.uk/author/rob-watsonwonderlearn-co-uk/ "Posts by Rob Watson") \| Feb 27, 2017 \| [Testimonials](https://wonderlearn.co.uk/category/testimonials/) \| [0 comments](https://wonderlearn.co.uk/charlotte-is-a-fantastic-tutor/#respond)

Charlotte is a fantastic tutor who created a strong rapport with my son from day one. She is brilliant at praising what is done well and positively encouraging improvement. She really helped my son improve his creative writing which he was struggling with. He is a typical 9 year old boy who would rather play on the Xbox than think about story mountains but after a few months of tutoring he was regularly writing stories marked A1 at school and went on to secure an academic scholarship at Whitgift.

F Khan, South Croydon
