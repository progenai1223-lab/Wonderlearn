    Privacy at Tillmore

    What we keep when you book an assessment or send an order, why, and for how long.

    What we collect

    • From the assessment form: your name, your email, your child's first name and school year.
    • From an order request: the same details, the items in your basket and the total.
    • In your browser: the basket itself, kept in this browser's local storage so it survives a page change. It never leaves your device until you send an order.

    We ask for no card details. Payment is made through a separate payment link we send by email.

    Why we use it

    To arrange the assessment, place your child in a group, confirm the places and courses you ordered, send the payment link and the receipt, and tell you about changes to your child's classes. That is our lawful basis: taking the steps you asked for before and under a contract with you.

    We never sell your details or use them for advertising, and this site sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

    Keeping and deleting your data

    Assessment notes are kept while your child attends and for one year after. Order records are kept for six years, because UK tax law asks us to keep sales records that long.

    You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what the law does not require us to keep. Write to us through the email link below and we answer within one month.

    To clear the basket from this browser, empty it or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

    Who sees it

    Our tutors and the centre's office. Our email provider stores the messages you send us. When online payment is connected, the payment provider will handle your card details on its own pages, under its own privacy notice; we never see your card number.

    Complaints

    If you are unhappy with how we use your data, tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data regulator.
