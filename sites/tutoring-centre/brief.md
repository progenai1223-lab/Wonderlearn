# Website brief

## The business

- **Name:** none yet. Coin one (search it first and keep the result in `names.json`). It should
  sound like a calm, serious local tutoring centre, not a franchise or an app. The folder slug stays
  `tutoring-centre`.
- **What is sold:** year-round tutoring for primary school children in Year 3, Year 4 and Year 5 (ages
  7 to 10). The centre builds strong learning habits through its own teaching approach, and prepares
  children for the 11 Plus (11+) grammar school entrance exams.
- **Who buys it:** parents in the UK who are considering grammar school for their child, and want
  steady preparation that starts early rather than a last-minute cram.
- **Kind:** decide by skill section 1. A tutoring centre that children attend and that teaches at its
  door is a **place**. Use the place plan unless the skill clearly says otherwise.

  `data-assumed`, and list it in the report.
- **Language of the site:** en-GB (British spelling: programme, practise, colour).

right group, and the parent gets feedback. This is the booking form.
