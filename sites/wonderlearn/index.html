    Lessons online, in person and on video

    Years 4 and 5 choose online or face-to-face lessons. Every other year group follows a blended model.

    • Regular online lessons

      Live on Zoom, at the same time each week of the term.

    • A meeting with your tutor

      A chance to sit down with Mrs Watson and discuss learning and progress.

    • Videos of lessons

      Watch a lesson again at home, at any time in the term.

    • Video tutorials

      Short tutorials that support each lesson's objectives.

    What every lesson teaches

    Reading actively, comprehension, creative writing, grammar and exam technique, taught by a qualified English teacher, so children enjoy and excel in English at 11+ and at GCSE.
