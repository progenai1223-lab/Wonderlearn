## The business

- **Name:** Wonderlearn. The strapline is "Enjoy and Excel". The name is real, so there's no coining
  and no `names.json` search.
- **Founder:** Mrs Charlotte Watson, a highly experienced and qualified English teacher.
- **What is sold:** English tuition: 11+ preparation (Years 3 to 6) and GCSE preparation (Years 7
  to 11), plus support for every part of reading and writing. Also workbooks, a video course, Year 5
  exam labs and creative writing masterclasses.
- **Who buys it:** parents of children in Years 3 to 11 (UK), mostly around Wallington, Croydon and
  Sutton, and online.
- **Kind:** decide by skill section 1. Lessons are mostly online (Zoom), with face-to-face classes
  for Years 4 and 5 at a hired church venue, so Wonderlearn has no door of its own. That reads as a
  **service** (people hire the tutor). Adapt the sections to the service plan where the skill
  requires it, but **keep the existing design system**: palette, type, radius, glass panels, motion,
  nav, footer and button style, exactly as the Tillmore pages have them. Don't re-deal the palette
  or the type.
- **Where:** online on Zoom, and face to face at Holy Trinity Church, Maldon Road, Wallington. House
  rule 9: street and district only, no postcode, so "Holy Trinity Church, Maldon Road, Wallington".
- **Contact:** mrswatson@wonderlearn.co.uk is the client's real, published address, so use it (not
  a `.example` stand-in). Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/wonderlearn.co.uk/. If a contact check
  FAILs on it, waive it with the reason "client's own published contact (brief)".
