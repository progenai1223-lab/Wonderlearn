# WONDERLEARN

# Enjoy and Excel

Wonderlearn is an effective and successful service for parents and pupils seeking excellence and experience in English tuition. It was founded by highly experienced and qualified English teacher, Mrs Charlotte Watson, to provide invaluable preparation for 11+ and GCSE exams or essential support to improve all aspects of reading and writing.

# TIMETABLE FOR REGULAR LESSONS FROM SEPTEMBER 2026 TO JULY 2027

# PRICES SHOWN FROM SEPTEMBER 2026

Wonderlearn proudly offers a choice of online or face to face lessons for years 4 and 5. All other year groups are offered an effective and successful blended learning model – regular online lessons with an opportunity to meet your tutor and discuss learning and progress. Videos of lessons are available as well as other video tutorials to support lesson objectives. All lessons meet high professional standards of teaching and learning, engaging pupils fully and challenging them to achieve their highest levels.
