    Privacy for Wonderlearn English tuition

    How Wonderlearn, English tuition for Years 3 to 11 on Zoom and in Wallington, handles what you send through the enquiry form and the checkout.

    Email Mrs Watson

    Data from enquiries and orders

    Details from an order or enquiry

    • From the enquiry form: your name, your email, your child's school year and the class you would like.
    • From an order request: your name and email, your child's first name and school year, the day you prefer, the items in your basket and the total.
    • In your browser: the basket itself, kept in this browser's local storage so it survives a page change. It never leaves your device until you send an order.

    How booking details are used

    To answer your enquiry, place your child in a class, confirm what you ordered, arrange payment, send downloads and tell you about changes to your child's lessons. That is our lawful basis: the steps you asked for, before and under a contract with you. We never sell your details or use them for advertising, and this site sets no tracking cookies.

    Your privacy rights and our records

    Enquiries and class details are kept while your child attends and for one year after. Order records are kept for six years, because UK tax law asks businesses to keep sales records that long. You can ask for a copy of what we hold, a correction, or deletion of what the law does not require us to keep, and Mrs Watson answers within one month.

    Privacy with email and payment providers

    Mrs Watson. Our email provider stores the messages you send. When online payment is connected, the payment provider handles card details on its own pages, under its own privacy notice.

    Contact for complaints

    Tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data regulator.
